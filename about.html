About BrightByte

A small Kuala Lumpur studio focused on clean web work.

Founded by Alham, Qistina, and Maira to provide quality web solutions at reasonable prices.

Our Story

Simple code, clear pricing, dependable websites.

BrightByte started after we noticed how difficult it was for local small businesses and campus organizations to get a proper website without paying huge agency retainers or wrestling with slow page builders.

Instead of overcomplicating projects with unnecessary plugins, we focus on solid fundamentals: semantic HTML, clean external CSS with responsive media queries, lightweight JavaScript, and straightforward service tables.
